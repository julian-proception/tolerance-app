What this assumes. Both dimensions are treated as independent, normally distributed and centred in their tolerance bands, with the band edges at ±3σ. Real machining often runs offset or skewed — tool wear drifts one way — so treat these percentages as an estimate of process capability, not a guarantee. The worst-case limits are what the drawing has to survive.

Sources

  • ISO 286-1:2010 — basis of the code system: IT grade definitions, fundamental deviation formulas, and the Δ rule this tool uses to derive hole deviations from shaft ones.
  • ISO 286-2:2010 — tables of limit deviations for hole and shaft classes.
  • Cross-checked against independently published reproductions: RoyMech ISO 286-2 hole and shaft tables, Engineers Edge IT grades, Coban Engineering shaft deviations, and Machinery's Handbook.
  • RSS stackup per standard tolerance-analysis practice — Fischer, Mechanical Tolerance Stackup and Analysis; Creveling, Tolerance Design.
  • See REFERENCES.md for which values rest on which source, and verify.html to re-run the checks in your browser.
